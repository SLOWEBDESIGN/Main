07 · Business Model

How 3DPrintDash earns

Operators supply the equipment. 3DPrintDash prices, collects payment, and coordinates the job.

Revenue stream Pricing today Role
Print service fees 24–38% of operator payout by tier, plus a $2 flat charge Primary transaction revenue
AI generation credits $15 for 3 creations · $30 for 8 · $60 for 20 Paid creation beyond free credits
Digital files $10 for a generated file with no physical print Serves design-only customers
Design services 28% fee on the designer's payout, plus a dispute reserve ~20% modeled contribution after costs
Color requests Greater of $3 or 5% of order value, split with the operator Covers added production cost

Both sides see the number before committing: the operator's payout, and the customer's price.

09 · Competition

A friendlier front door

3DPrintDash competes on the experience around manufacturing, not on production depth.

Alternative What they offer 3DPrintDash's edge
Protolabs CAD upload, part configuration, and manufacturing analysis for production requirements A guided entry from an idea, image, or model: the full purchase journey on a phone
Xometry Instant quoting on uploaded CAD, with broad industrial materials and processes Makes small custom jobs accessible alongside design creation and independent capacity
Craftcloud Price comparison across vetted manufacturing partners Creation, quoting, operator acceptance, and delivery in one experience
Treatstock A marketplace for printing, design, and manufacturing services One consistent mobile journey and a simple operator workflow
AI design tools Text-to-3D and image-to-3D generation tools and APIs Connects creation to a fulfilled physical order

The experience is the differentiator today; reliable fulfillment compounds it over time.
