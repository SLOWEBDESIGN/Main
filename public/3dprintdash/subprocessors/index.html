Subprocessors

Who else touches your data.

To run 3D Print Dash we rely on other companies, and some of them necessarily process customer data. This is the complete list. It is the same set named in our privacy policy, set out here in the form business customers and data-protection rules expect.

Some of these providers process data outside the United States, including in mainland China. Sending a description or photo to the AI features means accepting that transfer; you can use the service without them by uploading your own model file instead.

Stripe

Purpose. Payments, payouts and identity verification for operators

Data. Name, email, billing address, payment method token, transaction records

Location. United States

Supabase

Purpose. Database, authentication and file storage

Data. Account records, orders, uploaded and generated model files

Location. United States

Vercel

Purpose. Application hosting and delivery

Data. Request logs, IP address, user agent

Location. United States

Shippo

Purpose. Shipping rates, labels and tracking

Data. Recipient name, shipping address, parcel details

Location. United States

Resend

Purpose. Transactional email (order and payout notifications)

Data. Email address, message contents

Location. United States

Sentry

Purpose. Error monitoring

Data. Error traces with identifying fields scrubbed before they are sent

Location. United States

Google Analytics

Purpose. Product analytics, only with cookie consent

Data. Pseudonymous usage events, IP address

Location. United States

Tencent Hunyuan 3D

Purpose. Generating a 3D model from your description or photo

Data. The description you type and any photo you attach

Location. Mainland China

Zhipu AI (GLM)

Purpose. Improving and classifying your description, and drawing the concept image

Data. The description you type

Location. Mainland China

Google Gemini

Purpose. Fallback for improving and classifying your description

Data. The description you type

Location. United States

Stability AI

Purpose. Turning a sketch you draw into a concept image

Data. The sketch you draw

Location. United States

Tripo

Purpose. Generating a 3D model from multi-view sketches

Data. The sketches you draw

Location. United States

Cults3D

Purpose. The design library you can browse and pick from

Data. Your search terms

Location. France

Discord

Purpose. Operator community and job notifications, only if an operator links an account

Data. An operator’s Discord identity and the text of job notifications sent to them. No customer data is sent to Discord

Location. United States

Cloudflare Turnstile

Purpose. Bot protection on free generations

Data. IP address, browser signals

Location. United States

Operators are independent contractors, not subprocessors. An operator receives the model file and shipping address for a job they have accepted, because they cannot print and post the thing otherwise.
